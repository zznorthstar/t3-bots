## Separately installed dependencies

[Hermes Agent](https://github.com/NousResearch/hermes-agent) is developed by Nous Research and its contributors. Local speech uses [faster-whisper](https://github.com/SYSTRAN/faster-whisper) and [Kokoro ONNX](https://github.com/thewh1teagle/kokoro-onnx), with separately downloaded model and voice assets. Review their licenses and model cards before redistribution.
